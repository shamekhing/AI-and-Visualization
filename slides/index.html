4:15–4:30 · Two sides, one story

CLIP reads scenes, not places

Text side · Pair 1
  • Swapping the words moves the scores 482/500 dots shift.
  • 93 photos flip outright; the bias favours Australia.
Image side · Pair 2
  • Place-specific scenes separate cleanly (≤ 7% error).
  • Otherwise: scene stereotypes, biased towards Australia.
Convergent conclusionWithout place evidence, CLIP doesn't guess at random it falls back on postcard stereotypes, and the answer becomes unstable when you reword the prompt.

Phase I result
